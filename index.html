Privacy Policy — Skyscraper Town

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Skyscraper Town (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a single-player puzzle game published by PuzzleChef. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and who it is shared with. The game has no login, no user accounts, and does not ask you for your name, email, or any personal profile.

What we collect and why

The game itself stores your progress and coins only on your device. We do not upload your puzzle progress to any server.

The game includes two third-party services that collect data to keep the game free and to help us fix problems:

1. Advertising — Google AdMob

We show ads (rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between levels). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address, for ad serving;
  • information about how you interact with ads.

This data is used to serve and measure ads. In regions that require it (such as the EEA and UK), the game shows a consent screen before any personalised ads are served, and you can reopen your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, the game still works.

2. Analytics & crash reporting — Google Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played in aggregate and to detect crashes. These may collect:

  • app activity (in-app events such as starting and finishing a level);
  • device and other identifiers and basic device/app information;
  • crash logs and diagnostics if the game crashes.

This data is used for analytics and to improve stability. It is not used to identify you personally.

Data sharing

Data described above is shared with Google as the provider of AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Crashlytics, acting as our service providers / advertising partners. We do not sell your data. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob & consent: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7666366
  • Firebase privacy & security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Children

Skyscraper Town is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Reset or reopen your personalised-ads consent: Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation: your device’s system settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
